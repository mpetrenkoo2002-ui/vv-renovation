About the Project

Woodwork That Follows the Forest

At Moutcha Bay Resort, our carpentry work focused on the outdoor connections around the forest cabins: timber walkways, steps, landings and wooden railings. The layout follows the cabins and the hillside, with decking fitted around the existing trees.

The site photos follow the work from exposed timber framing and on-site cutting through to deck-board installation and railing assembly. Individual cuts around trunks, posts and cabin corners connect these separate details into one continuous timber route.

Scope & Craftsmanship

The Carpentry Scope

The work brings together the supporting timber, walking surfaces and railing details around the cabins. Each part is fitted to the changes in level and the surrounding site.

01

Timber Walkway Framing

Supporting timber framing for the outdoor walkways and landings, shaped to follow the cabin edges and the hillside layout.

02

Decking & Walking Surfaces

Deck-board installation along the paths and cabin approaches, including the cuts needed around posts, corners and existing trees.

03

Wooden Stairs & Landings

Steps and timber landings connecting different levels along the outdoor routes beside the cabins.

04

Railings & Wood Details

Wooden posts, rails and vertical railing members fitted along the walkways and stair sections.

Working With the Site

Cabin entrances, trees and changes in ground level all influence the route. The timber work has to follow these conditions rather than repeat a single straight layout.

Fitted on Site

The framing, deck boards and railing sections were cut and assembled around the actual spaces between cabins and trees. The photos show these details taking shape throughout the work.
