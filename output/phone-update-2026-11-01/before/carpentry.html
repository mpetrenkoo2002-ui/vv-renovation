What We Do

Woodwork for Everyday Spaces

From a timber walkway to the finishing details inside a room, good carpentry brings a space together. VV Renovation installs and repairs decks, stairs, railings and interior wood details for residential and commercial renovation projects.

Based in Richmond and serving Greater Vancouver, we work with you to plan the layout, choose materials and fit each detail to the space.

Our Specialties

Carpentry We Can Help With

Decks & Walkways

Installation and replacement of decking and timber walkways, fitted to the building and the surrounding site.

Stairs & Railings

Wooden steps, handrails and railing details for entrances, decks and outdoor connections.

Wood Repairs

Repair and replacement of worn or damaged wood as part of a renovation, with the scope assessed on site.

Interior Trim

Baseboards, door and window trim, and other finishing details with careful cuts and neat joints.

Custom Wood Details

Wood features and fitted details planned around your layout, materials and renovation goals.

Renovation Carpentry

Practical woodwork coordinated with the other trades, from preparation through to the finishing stage.
