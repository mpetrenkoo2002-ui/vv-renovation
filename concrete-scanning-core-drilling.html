05 / Questions & Answers

FAQ

What is concrete scanning used for?+

Scanning helps identify reinforcement and other embedded features in the work area before drilling or cutting. What can be detected depends on the equipment, concrete and site conditions; a scan is not a guarantee that every embedded object will be found.

Can I request scanning and drilling together?+

Yes. Choose “Scanning + core drilling” in the quote form and describe the work. You can also request either service separately.

What information do you need for a quote?+

Start with the site location and the service you need. For drilling, include the number and approximate diameter of openings, concrete thickness if known, and whether the work is in a wall or floor. Photos, drawings and access details help us review the request.

Can you confirm the opening size from photos?+

Photos help with an initial review, but may not show the concrete thickness, access constraints or what is embedded inside. Required dimensions and site conditions need to be confirmed before the work is agreed.

What areas do you serve?+

VV Renovation serves Greater Vancouver. Include the site city or address in your request so we can discuss the location and scheduling.
