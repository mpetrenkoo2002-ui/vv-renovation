About the Project

Preparing a Luxury Jericho Beach Estate for Summer

VV Renovation prepared a luxury residential estate in Jericho Beach, Vancouver, getting the multi-level home — with its curved architecture, indoor pool, home gym, wine room and tennis court — fully ready for the summer season.

The scope covered interior painting of curved walls and ceilings, stucco crack repair and exterior repainting, concrete crack repair and walkway recoating, hot tub assembly, outdoor panel refinishing, lock replacement and a range of detailed handyman tasks — all completed to a high standard within a tight timeline.

Scope, Materials & Methods

What We Completed

This Jericho Beach estate project combined interior painting of curved walls and ceilings, stucco and concrete crack repair, exterior repainting and walkway recoating, hot tub assembly, outdoor panel refinishing and detailed handyman work to prepare the home for seasonal occupancy.

01

Interior Painting

Painted curved walls and ceilings throughout the home, carefully masking porthole windows, glass partitions and feature surfaces to keep clean lines across the estate's non-standard architecture.

02

Stucco Crack Repair & Exterior Repainting

Repaired cracks in the exterior stucco and repainted the affected areas to restore a clean, consistent and well-maintained appearance to the property's facade.

03

Concrete Crack Repair & Walkway Recoating

Repaired cracks in the exterior concrete and recoated the walkways, giving the surrounding hard surfaces a refreshed, uniform and durable finish.

04

Hot Tub Assembly & Positioning

Assembled the hot tub, connected its mechanical components and positioned the unit in place, ready for use in the estate's indoor spa area.

05

Outdoor Panel Refinishing

Refinished outdoor panels on the terrace, restoring their look and protecting them for ongoing use in the property's exterior areas.

06

Locks, Windows & Handyman Work

Replaced locks, adjusted windows and completed a range of detailed handyman tasks to resolve deficiencies and make the home fully functional and move-in ready.

The Challenge

The estate's curved walls, angled ceilings and non-standard architectural details made painting and finishing far more demanding than a standard home. On top of that, interior painting, exterior repairs and mechanical work all had to be coordinated together on a tight seasonal timeline.

The Solution

We organized the work by zone, masking each curved surface individually for clean, precise paint lines, and sequenced the interior, exterior and mechanical tasks so trades never collided. The result was a high-quality finish across the whole property, completed on schedule.

Final Result

An Estate Fully Prepared for Summer

The estate was delivered fully prepared for summer occupancy — with a freshly painted interior and exterior, repaired and recoated walkways, an assembled hot tub and every deficiency resolved. The home was handed back move-in ready within one month.
