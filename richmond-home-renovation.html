Planning for the property

Richmond condo, townhome and house renovations

Every property has different access, working-hour and approval requirements. For a condo or townhome, share the strata bylaws, renovation application and elevator or loading arrangements early. For a detached home, identify any layout changes, demolition and older materials that may affect the scope.

If a permit, engineering review or licensed trade is required, that needs to be confirmed before the affected work begins. Clear information at the estimate stage helps reduce surprises later.

  • Property type and Richmond neighbourhood
  • Rooms, approximate size and desired finish
  • Photos, drawings or inspiration references
  • Building access, strata rules and preferred timing
