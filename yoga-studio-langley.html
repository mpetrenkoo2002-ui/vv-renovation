Scope, Materials & Methods

What We Completed

This Langley yoga studio project included structural preparation, drywall and ceiling work, self-leveling, vinyl tile installation, yoga mat installation, decorative wall panels, furniture assembly and detailed finishing around mirrors, baseboards and training areas.

01

Framing, Studs & Drywall

Completed framing work using steel/wood studs, then installed drywall to prepare the studio layout for ceiling work, wall finishes and final interior details.

02

Ceiling Work & Studio Preparation

Worked on the studio ceiling system and prepared the space for the next finishing stages, including lighting areas, wall connections and clean interior transitions.

03

Self-Leveling & Floor Preparation

Prepared the floor with self-leveling before the vinyl tile and yoga mat installation, creating a smoother base for the final studio flooring.

04

Vinyl Tile & Yoga Mat Installation

Installed vinyl tiles and yoga mats for the main studio areas, creating a durable training surface suitable for daily yoga, fitness and studio use.

05

Decorative Wall Panels

Installed decorative wall panels to add texture, warmth and a more finished interior look to the studio, including feature areas around the training space.

06

Furniture, Shelves & Small Installations

Completed storage assembly, furniture installation, shelves, outlet-related details and small handyman tasks needed to make the studio functional and ready for use.

The Challenge

The most difficult detail was the baseboard work around the mirror wall and yoga mat areas. The baseboards had been installed before the mirrors and yoga mats were fully completed, so the final floor height and mirror clearance no longer aligned cleanly.

The Solution

We removed the affected baseboards, cut each piece to fit the new height and reinstalled them section by section between the mirrors and yoga mats. This created a cleaner transition around the wall, mat edges and surrounding finishes.

Final Result

A Finished Studio Ready for Daily Use

The completed Yoga 7 studio now has a clean training area, installed yoga mats, a mirror wall, decorative wall panels, storage shelving and finished interior details ready for daily client use.
